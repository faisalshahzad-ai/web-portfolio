Questions

What is AI automation?

It combines workflow software (triggers, APIs and business rules) with AI models that can read text, classify requests and draft replies, so repetitive manual work disappears.

How long does a project take?

A focused starter workflow usually moves from discovery to production in a few weeks. Larger projects are delivered in milestones so you get value early.

Do people stay in control?

Yes. Human-in-the-loop steps send risky or unclear cases to a person for approval before anything happens.
